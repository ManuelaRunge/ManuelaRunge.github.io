This study developed a stakeholder-driven research agenda to support policy and decision-making for future Lassa fever vaccine introduction in West Africa.
Using the Child Health and Nutrition Research Initiative methodology, 235 experts prioritised research questions across evidence needs including vaccination target groups, vaccine performance in special populations, economic impact, and vaccine acceptance.
The resulting agenda identifies critical evidence gaps to help align research generation with national, regional, and global vaccine policy needs.
