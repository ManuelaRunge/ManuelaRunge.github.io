This article describes the implementation and evaluation of two iterations of a faculty enrichment program in applied malaria modeling.
The program combined technical modeling skills, scientific communication, and malaria epidemiology training for scholars from Sub-Saharan African institutions.
Participant and instructor interviews highlighted gains in technical expertise, research and communication skills, opportunities for knowledge transfer, and the importance of continuous feedback mechanisms in contextually relevant training programs.
