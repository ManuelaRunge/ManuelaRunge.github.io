This implementation study reviews six rounds of Tanzania’s School Malaria Parasitological Survey between 2014 and 2025 and their contribution to council-level malaria surveillance, stratification and intervention planning.

It examines how government ownership, local delivery, consistent methods, integrated data systems and sustained financing support a national school-based surveillance programme.
